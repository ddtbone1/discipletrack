## 1. What the material is

- **Title and authors.** "Journey: The Spiritual Formation of Multiplying Disciples" by John and Cathy Honeycutt. TheJourneyForum.com and DiscipleThePlanet.com are cited throughout.
- **First of four levels.** Lesson 10 describes these lessons as "the first of four levels of training".
- **No copyright notice.** None of Lessons 2 to 10 carries a copyright, licence or reproduction notice. Lesson 8 has a usage instruction, "Do not use the lessons ... for any other purpose than one on one discipleship", but that is not a licence.
- **This is the Discipler's edition.** Lesson 1 is headed "DISCIPLERS' COPY" on both the cover and the first page. The page images show every blank answered in blue, and every "write the verse" exercise written out. The text layer has the blanks and none of the answers. So the source holds both a blank workbook (the text layer) and an answer key (the overlay).

## 5. Implications for the curriculum structure (observations, not decisions)

1. **Licensing comes before structure.** ADR-010 decision 1 keeps the lesson material as Markdown in the repository and publishes it to Supabase for every ACTIVE member to read. That means reproducing a third-party, apparently commercial curriculum in the repository and in the app. The source has no licence notice, so the right to do that has to be established with the publisher first. It also cuts against keeping the PDFs out of git.
2. **Two content tiers exist in the source.** The blank workbook (text layer) suits a Disciple. The answers, written verses and Training Modules suit the Discipler. ADR-010 gives every ACTIVE member the same read scope, and ADR-013 (reserved, Slice 9) is where Guide-only protection was expected. Slice 7 cannot publish the answer-filled edition under ADR-010's scope without exposing the answer key.
3. **The progress model needs no change.** One completion per lesson, marked by the Discipler (ADR-015), corresponds to the Assignments-page "Date completed / Discipler signature". Lessons are sequential, as the source assumes.
4. **Several elements are workbook features, not reading content.** These are the blanks, verse writing, homework answers, the Daily in the Word notebook, memory-verse tracking, self-assessment and the module sign-offs. Storing a person's responses is ADR-013 and Slice 9 territory (D17), not Slice 7.
5. **The structure is regular enough to model.** Lesson, then sections (title, key objective, banners), then ordered blocks: paragraph, scripture reference, fill-in statement, discussion prompts, scenario, verse-writing exercise, assignment list, module. If the content is in scope, it can be held as structured data rather than free Markdown.
6. **The guide fixes how the material is used in a meeting** (section 6). In particular, the Discipler holds the answers and gives them during the meeting, and the disciple must not work ahead. That bears directly on who may read what, and when.

## 6. The Lesson 1 guide (pages 1 to 6)

Facts as stated in the guide. Short phrases are quoted; the rest is summarized.

**Getting started**
- Register at TheJourneyForum.com: "Every page of every lesson has resources on the website."
- The contents page lists the ten lessons with themes and topics: Salvation, Assurance, Prayer, Local Church, Discipleship, The Future, Spiritual Growth, Faith, Words, Loving God. Training Modules 1 to 7 are attached to Lessons 5 to 9.

**Pairing**
- "Men meet with men. Women with women. And the church leadership decides who meets with whom."
- "Close family members and best friends should only meet together with their Pastor's approval."

**Cadence and pacing**
- "Meet together at least once every week". Schedule "catch-up" meetings when a week is missed, and "never have long periods of time where you don't meet together."
- If they meet daily, "study the lessons in Journey only every 2 or 3 days", using the other days for the Daily in the Word assignments.
- No number of meetings per lesson is given.

**Working on the lessons**
- "The disciple only work on the lessons with their Discipler and not in advance." The disciple may read the verses ahead "but should not write answers in the blanks ahead of a meeting."
- "Disciplers should not add their own materials or topics."
- Conversations are kept confidential unless something is life threatening or very serious.

**Lesson One**
- Lesson One "is designed to lead people to Christ or to teach a disciple a complete study on Salvation. You can begin Journey with a believer or non-believer."

**A typical meeting**
1. Talk first ("there are times you will not even open the lessons").
2. Exchange Daily in the Word and other assignments. "The Discipler reviews and signs that they were completed."
3. The Discipler prays and reviews what was learned before.
4. "The Discipler reads any introductions and summaries and the disciple reads the Bible verses." Then "the Discipler reads the comments and gives the answers for the blanks and the disciple writes the answers in their lesson."
5. Follow the built-in instructions in order: "do not skip any section, page, or assignment." Do the Reflect and Transfer at the end of each section, and all assignments at the end of each lesson.
6. The Discipler reviews, schedules the next meeting and records it: "record your meeting in your records (see your church leaders for their record keeping method)". The meeting closes with prayer.

**Discipler preparation**
- The Discipler prepares by knowing the context of the verses and the meaning of key words.
- The icon marks extra Discipler guidance that lives on the website, not in the lesson.

## 7. Lesson 1 content (from the text)

- **Section A** (pages 2 to 7): who God is (creator, triune), the origin of evil (Lucifer), the creation of man in God's image, the fall and sin.
  - About 50 fill-in blanks; some answers have alternatives ("wicked or evil", "reproduce or increase").
  - Reflect and Transfer: explain 1 John 5:7. Water cooler: suffering and disasters. Write Romans 5:12.
- **Section B** (pages 8 to 13): the promise of a Saviour (Genesis 3:15), a summary of the Ten Commandments, prophecy, a narrative summary of the life, death and resurrection of Jesus, salvation by faith, and a suggested prayer of salvation.
  - About 30 blanks. Reflect and Transfer: explain Romans 10:13. Water cooler: Jesus as only a good teacher, and exclusivity. Write Romans 5:8.
- **Page 14:** on the Word of God and Step 2. Page 15: online resources, including the "Spiritual Map", "a summary of lesson one to use as a witnessing tool".
- **Page 16, assignments:**
  - Set up a ring notebook with sections Daily in the Word, Prayer, Special Verses, Message Notes and Bible Study.
  - Answer 10 questions, one of them multiple choice, on separate paper.
  - Write answers to the water cooler questions.
  - Date completed / Discipler signature.
- **Same patterns as Lessons 2 to 10:** sections with banners, scripture-anchored fill-in statements, Reflect and Transfer, water cooler, verse writing, assignments, Discipler signature. Lesson 1 has no Training Module.
